FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Which Florida cities does BBQ Carioca serve?

We serve all of South Florida, including Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Palm Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Miami, West Palm Beach, and surrounding areas. For corporate or larger events, we also serve Orlando.

What is included in the catering packages?

Our packages include a professional Pitmaster (Chef Bruno), charcoal grill setup, prime beef cuts (Picanha, Top Sirloin, Ribs, Sausages), appetizers (garlic bread), traditional sides (farofa, vinaigrette, rice), premium disposable plates & cutlery, and grill area cleanup.

How long does the BBQ service last during the event?

We arrive 2 hours prior to serving time to set up and start the fire. The continuous live barbecue buffet service lasts 3 to 4 hours for your guests.

What is the minimum and maximum guest capacity?

We cater from small backyard parties (starting at 15 guests) to large corporate functions, birthdays, and weddings with over 200 guests.

How does date reservation and payment work?

To reserve your event date, we require a 20% deposit. The remaining balance is paid on the day of the event. We accept Zelle, Credit Cards, Venmo, and Cash.

What if it rains on the day of my event?

Our team provides professional rain tents for the grill area. The fire stays hot and your event runs smoothly regardless of weather!
