Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: August 18, 2026 · Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Accessibility

BBQ do Carioca is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for individuals with disabilities. We continuously improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards under Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 AA).

2. Standards & Measures Applied

We have implemented the following technical accessibility measures across our web pages:

  • Semantic Markup: Structured HTML5 tags (`

  • Keyboard Navigation: All interactive buttons, form controls, and links are accessible via standard keyboard navigation.
  • Contrast Ratios: High contrast color pairings exceeding 4.5:1 ratio for text legibility.
  • Alternative Text: Descriptive `alt` attributes on all images and visual media.
  • ARIA Attributes: Proper ARIA roles and state indicators (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`) for interactive UI components.
