Tracking Transparency

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026 · Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage are small text files or data entries placed on your browser when visiting websites. They help remember your preferences, keep your session active, and provide insights into site performance.

2. How BBQ do Carioca Uses Cookies

We keep tracking to an absolute minimum to respect user privacy. We utilize the following categories of storage:

  • Strictly Necessary Local Storage (`bbq-lang`): Stores your selected language preference (English or Portuguese) on your browser so you do not have to select it again on return visits.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Measures aggregate website traffic patterns, visitor counts across Florida cities, and device types to ensure site speed and usability.

3. Managing and Opting Out

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your web browser settings (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Firefox). Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to view our menu or submit quote requests via WhatsApp.
