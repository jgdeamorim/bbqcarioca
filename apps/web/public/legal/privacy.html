2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or describes you ("Personal Information"). We adhere to the principle of data minimization—only collecting data necessary to deliver our catering services.

Information You Provide Directly

  • Identity Data: Full name, company name (if corporate event).
  • Contact Data: Phone number (for WhatsApp quote response), email address, event location/city.
  • Event Specifications: Date of event, estimated guest count, grill availability, meat preferences, and custom notes.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Technical Identifiers: IP address, browser type, operating system, device characteristics.
  • Usage Data: Referring URL, pages visited, time spent per section, button clicks, and scroll depth.
  • Cookies & Tracking: Session state, language preferences (EN/PT), and aggregate web analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your Personal Information solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • Quote Fulfillment: Processing your catering inquiries and building customized event proposals.
  • Direct Communication: Contacting you via WhatsApp or phone regarding your event date and logistics.
  • Service Delivery: Managing on-site churrasco catering, team dispatch, and grill setup.
  • Website Optimization: Analyzing site usability, system performance, and accessibility.
  • Legal & Fraud Prevention: Complying with U.S. tax, accounting, and legal obligations, and protecting against unauthorized transactions.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We utilize essential local storage keys (such as storing your language preference: `bbq-lang`) and standard web analytics cookies to measure site performance. We do not engage in unauthorized cross-site data broker tracking.

5. Third-Party Services & How We Share Information

We do NOT sell your Personal Information for monetary compensation. We share data only with trusted service providers necessary to operate our business under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Communication Partners: WhatsApp (Meta Business API) for instant quote communication.
  • Hosting & Edge Infrastructure: Cloudflare Pages (Edge network hosting and security protection).
  • Analytics & Performance: Aggregated web analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Legal Mandates: Disclosure when required by law enforcement, court order, or regulatory authorities.

7. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard physical, technical, and administrative safeguards (including HTTPS TLS 1.3 encryption, access controls, and regular data purging). 8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our website is a commercial catering service intended for adult event hosts (aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 13 in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Our website and servers are operated in the United States. If you access our site from outside the U.S. (including Brazil, Canada, or the EU), your information will be transferred, processed, and stored in the United States under U.S. privacy laws.
